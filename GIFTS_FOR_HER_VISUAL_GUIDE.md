## 🎯 Interactive Elements

### Hover Effects
- Product cards: Scale + shadow-xl
- Price cards: Scale(1.05) + border-pink
- Featured cards: Border-pink
- Buttons: Darker shade transition
- Images: Scale(1.1) or swap

### Click Behaviors
- Occasion chips: Filter products + pink bg
- Price cards: Filter + scroll to grid
- Wishlist: Toggle heart fill
- Add to cart: Add product (integration needed)
- CTAs: Smooth scroll to grid

### Mobile Gestures
- Occasion strip: Horizontal scroll
- Featured section: Swipe cards
- Product grid: Vertical scroll
- Sticky CTA: Tap to scroll

---

## ✨ Micro-interactions

1. **Occasion Selector**: Scale animation on active
2. **Price Cards**: Heart icon subtle pulse
3. **Product Images**: 300ms fade on hover swap
4. **Wishlist**: Heart fill animation
5. **Sticky CTA**: Slide-up from bottom
6. **Add to Cart**: Button loading state
7. **Scroll Reveals**: Fade-in animations (optional)

---

## 🔗 Internal Linking

All components scroll to `#shop-gifts` anchor on CTA clicks for seamless UX.
