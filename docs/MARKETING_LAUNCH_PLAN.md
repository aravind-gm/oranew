# ORA Jewellery — Launch Marketing Plan
## Budget: ₹300/day (~₹9,000/month)

## 🎯 Phase 1: Pre-Launch Buzz (Days 1–7)
**Budget: ₹0 (organic only)**

### What to Do
1. **Instagram Setup** — Post 9 grid photos (flat-lays, lifestyle shots, close-ups)
2. **Reels (3–5):**
   - Unboxing reel (show packaging → product reveal)
   - "₹499 vs ₹5,000 — spot the difference" (ORA vs gold jewellery)
   - "3 ways to style this necklace" outfit transitions
3. **WhatsApp Broadcast** — Tell friends, family, colleagues. Ask them to follow + share.
4. **Stories** — Behind-the-scenes (packing orders, quality checks)

### Free Site Reveal Video
Use one of these **100% free tools** to create a site walkthrough/reveal video:
- **Loom** (loom.com) — Free screen recorder, record your site walkthrough, add voiceover
- **OBS Studio** (obsproject.com) — Free & open-source, professional quality screen recording
- **ScreenPal** (screenpal.com) — Free tier, easy editing, add text overlays
- **Canva Video Editor** — Use the free tier to add text, music, transitions to your screen recording

**Best approach:** Record screen with Loom → edit in Canva → post as Instagram Reel + Story

## 📱 Phase 2: Launch Week (Days 8–14)
**Budget: ₹300/day = ₹2,100/week**

### Ad Split
| Platform | Daily | Weekly | Purpose |
|----------|-------|--------|---------|
| **Instagram/Meta Ads** | ₹200 | ₹1,400 | Awareness + Traffic |
| **Google Shopping** | ₹100 | ₹700 | High-intent buyers |

### Instagram/Meta Ads Strategy
**Campaign 1: Catalog / Carousel Ad (₹150/day)**
- **Objective:** Traffic → orashop.in
- **Audience:** Women 18–35, India, interests: fashion jewellery, GIVA, Caratlane, online shopping
- **Creative:** Carousel showing 4 bestsellers with prices
- **Copy:** "Jewellery that doesn't fade. Starting ₹399. Free delivery 🇮🇳"
- **CTA:** Shop Now

**Campaign 2: Reel/Story Ad (₹50/day)**
- **Objective:** Engagement → Video views
- **Creative:** Your best-performing organic reel (re-use)
- **Audience:** Same as above + Lookalike of website visitors
- **Purpose:** Build retargeting audience for later

### Google Shopping Setup
- **Budget:** ₹100/day
- **Feed:** Use Google Merchant Center (free) → connect your product feed
- **Target:** India, women searching "buy necklace online", "affordable rings", "gift for girlfriend"
- **Bidding:** Maximize clicks (start), switch to Target ROAS after 50 conversions

## 🔥 Phase 3: Growth Mode (Days 15–30)
**Budget: ₹300/day**

### Optimised Split (after 2 weeks of data)
| Platform | Daily | Purpose |
|----------|-------|---------|
| **Meta — Retargeting** | ₹100 | Website visitors, cart abandoners |
| **Meta — Prospecting** | ₹100 | New audiences, lookalikes |
| **Google Shopping** | ₹100 | Search + Shopping |

### Key Optimisations
1. **Kill underperformers** — Turn off any ad with CTR < 1% or CPC > ₹15
2. **Scale winners** — If an ad gets CPC < ₹5 and purchases, increase budget to ₹150/day
3. **Retargeting audiences:**
   - Visited site but didn't buy (last 7 days)
   - Added to cart but didn't checkout (last 3 days)
   - Engaged with Instagram (last 14 days)

## 🥤 Tumbler/Mug Marketing Strategy

### Why Tumblers?
- **Lower price point** → Easier first purchase
- **Gifting angle** → "Perfect gift under ₹999"
- **Viral potential** → Unboxing + "dupe" content performs well
- **Cross-sell** → Tumbler buyer → jewellery buyer

### Tumbler-Specific Content Plan
1. **Reel:** "Stanley dupe that's actually good" — Side-by-side comparison
2. **Reel:** "Ice test — 24 hours later" — Proof of insulation
3. **Story:** "Pack an order with me" — Show tumbler packaging
4. **Carousel post:** "5 reasons to switch to a reusable tumbler"

### Tumbler Ad Creative
- **Hook:** "This ₹799 tumbler keeps ice for 24 hours"
- **Body:** Show the ice test, show the colours, show it in a bag
- **CTA:** "Free delivery. Shop now at orashop.in"
- **Target audience:** Women 18–30, interests: fitness, sustainability, Stanley, coffee
