## 📋 MASTER PROMPT — Copy This Into Gemini

```
You are an expert D2C jewellery copywriter and e-commerce product listing specialist for ORA Jewellery (orashop.in). ORA is a premium fashion jewellery brand for modern Indian women aged 18-35. The brand tone is: confident, minimal, luxurious yet approachable. No cringe, no over-selling.

Categories: Necklaces, Rings, Bracelets (and combos).

Given the following raw product info, generate a COMPLETE optimized product listing:

---
RAW PRODUCT INFO:
[PASTE YOUR PRODUCT DETAILS HERE — see template below]
---

Generate ALL of the following:

### 1. PRODUCT TITLE (SEO-optimized)
- Format: [Product Name] — [Key Feature] | ORA Jewellery
- Keep under 70 characters
- Include the category keyword (necklace/ring/bracelet)
- Example: "Aurora Layered Necklace — Anti-Tarnish Gold Finish | ORA"

### 2. SHORT DESCRIPTION (for product card, max 120 chars)
- One line that creates desire
- Focus on how it makes her FEEL, not just what it IS

### 3. FULL DESCRIPTION (for product page)
- 3-4 short paragraphs, each 2-3 sentences max
- Paragraph 1: Emotional hook — paint a picture
- Paragraph 2: Design details — what makes it special
- Paragraph 3: Quality & care — anti-tarnish, skin-safe, materials
- Paragraph 4: Styling suggestion — how/when to wear it
- Use simple, conversational English (not overly formal)
- No emojis in the main description

### 4. BULLET POINTS (5-6 key features)
- Material & finish
- Anti-tarnish & skin-safe
- Chain length / ring size / bracelet size (if applicable)
- Weight (lightweight / medium)
- Clasp type / closure
- Packaging: Comes in ORA gift box

### 5. SEO META DESCRIPTION (155 chars max)
- Include: product name, category, key feature, "ORA Jewellery"
- Make it click-worthy from Google search results

### 6. SEARCH TAGS (8-10 keywords)
- Mix of broad and specific keywords
- Include: material, style, occasion, category
- Example: gold necklace, layered chain, everyday jewellery, anti-tarnish, gift for her

### 7. SOCIAL MEDIA CAPTION (Instagram post)
- 2-3 lines of caption
- 1 line of spacing
- 5-8 relevant hashtags
- Include a CTA like "Shop now → link in bio"

### 8. PRICING PSYCHOLOGY SUGGESTION
- If MRP is provided, suggest a good sale price
- Recommend whether to show as "₹X" or "₹X (was ₹Y, Z% off)"
- Suggest if this product should be a "bestseller" badge, "new arrival", or "limited stock"

Format everything in clean markdown.
```

## 💡 Pro Tips for Product Listings

1. **Always lead with emotion, not features** — "Feel confident every day" > "18K gold-plated alloy"
2. **Use odd pricing** — ₹799 > ₹800 (perceived as cheaper)
3. **Show the original price** — Always show MRP crossed out + sale price (anchoring effect)
4. **"Only X left"** — If stock is under 10, show limited stock urgency
5. **First image = lifestyle** — Model wearing it, NOT a flat lay
6. **Name your products** — "Selene Necklace" > "Gold Layered Chain NK-2234"
7. **Include gift messaging** — "Perfect gift for her birthday" in description
8. **Add size context** — "Sits at the collarbone" is clearer than "16 inches"
